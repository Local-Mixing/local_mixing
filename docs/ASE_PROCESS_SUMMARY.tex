\begin{abstract}
This document collects what we currently understand about the random process that DB-substitution
mixing induces on the space of reversible circuits: \textbf{(a)} the theory of \emph{Aggregate
Short-Excursion} (ASE) processes --- what they are, the stationary distribution they appear to
converge to, and what is known about their mixing time; \textbf{(b)} the evidence that our DB-based
mixing \emph{is} an ASE process; \textbf{(c)} the measured distribution of the per-position
displacement $\Di$ for the band-restricted experiments, via two independent instruments (Hamming
distance and the degree-1 affine predictor); and \textbf{(d)} the conclusions on the \emph{effective}
$\Di$ for the production pipeline (run\_c/run\_d), whose raw-Hamming prefix analysis was done in an
adjacent session. All figures/tables are reproduced; every external work is annotated in the
References.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Notation}
Let $C=g_1\cdots g_m$ be the \textbf{original} circuit (word over a fixed set $S$ of small reversible
gates on $2N$ wires; logical width $N$) and $M=g'_1\cdots g'_{m'}$ the \textbf{mixed} circuit,
$M\equiv C$. Prefix products $P_i=g_1\cdots g_i$, $Q_j=g'_1\cdots g'_j$. The central observable is the
\textbf{displacement}
\[
  D_i = M_i\circ P_{i'}^{-1},\qquad \Di=\text{minimal \# gates realizing }D_i,
\]
with $P_{i'}$ the aligned original prefix (argmin over $i'$ = transverse; or proportional
$i'/m=i/m'$). Two instruments estimate $\Di$: \textbf{Hamming}
$H(i,j)=\mathbb{E}_x\,\mathrm{ham}(P_i(x),Q_j(x))$ (bits of $2N$ wires; certified $\Di\ge H$), and
\textbf{affine} $H_{\mathrm{aff}}(i,j)\in[0,\tfrac12]$ (best degree-1 GF(2) reconstruction error;
blind to affine displacement, so it reads the \emph{nonlinear} part).

\section{Theory of ASE processes}

\subsection{Definition}\label{ss:def}
An \textbf{Aggregate Short-Excursion} process is a random walk on the \emph{fiber}
$\Phi_C=\{w:\mathrm{eval}(w)=C\}$ whose move is a \textbf{relator substitution}: replace a short
subword computing a permutation $\pi$ by a different short word computing the same $\pi$. Two views:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Configuration:} formally a \emph{fiber walk with a finite relator menu} --- the
non-commutative counterpart of a fiber walk with a Markov basis [DS98]; the precise dictionary,
including where the analogy is a theorem and where it breaks, is \S\ref{ss:markov}--\S\ref{ss:transport}.
Each move inserts a relator (a short loop at the identity in the Cayley graph).
\item \textbf{Trajectory:} through prefix products the mixed word is a path pinned at both ends
($Q_0=e$, $Q_{m'}=C$); a substitution is a heat-bath redraw of a short interior segment given its two
pinned endpoints --- a \emph{random-walk bridge}. The full trajectory is a \textbf{concatenation of
many short returning bridges}: aggregate short excursions.
\end{itemize}
The name isolates what separates an ASE process from a free walk: a free walk in nonamenable geometry
diverges and essentially never returns, whereas every ASE excursion is \emph{forced} to return. (The
free baseline chain whose bridges these excursions are is the random-gate walk on the permutation
group [Gow96].)

\subsection{The commutative template: fiber walks with Markov bases, precisely}\label{ss:markov}
The Markov-basis program of algebraic statistics is the one setting where ``walk on the fiber of a
conservation law with a fixed finite move menu'' is a developed theory; this section states it at
theorem level so every transported claim can be audited. (Primary sources re-verified: [DS98, ALP23,
SS03, DLO06a, DLO06b, Win16, SW18].)

\textbf{Setup and the two walks.} For $A\in\mathbb{Z}^{d\times n}$ the fiber
$\mathcal{F}_b=\{x\in\mathbb{N}^n: Ax=b\}$ is the lattice-point set of a polytope; statistically the
support of the conditional law given the sufficient statistic of a log-linear model (the generalized
hypergeometric, [DS98, Eq.~1.4]). A \emph{move} is $m\in\ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A)$; the \emph{basic fiber
walk} proposes $x\pm m$ from a finite menu $\mathcal{M}$ and holds when the proposal goes negative ---
symmetric proposal $\Rightarrow$ reversible, uniform-stationary on its communicating class
[DS98, Lem.~2.1]. Already in [DS98, Lem.~2.2]: the \emph{line heat-bath}, resampling the entire ray
$(x+\mathbb{Z}m)\cap\mathcal{F}_b$ --- DS remark it ``obviously gets random much more rapidly'' but
proving so is open: the earliest form of the resampling-length lever of \S\ref{ss:mix}.

\textbf{The Fundamental Theorem [DS98, Thm.~3.1].} $\mathcal{M}$ is a \emph{Markov basis} (connects
every fiber through nonnegative partial sums --- a path \emph{inside} the fiber) iff the binomials
$\{x^{m^+}-x^{m^-}: m\in\mathcal{M}\}$ \emph{generate the toric ideal} $I_A$. A move \emph{is} a
binomial; applicability is monomial divisibility; a connecting path is a telescoped ideal-membership
certificate whose multiplier monomials are the contexts at which moves apply --- exactly the object
whose non-commutative replacement (conjugators in an identity sequence) has no finite theory
(\S\ref{ss:transport}). Every $A$ has a finite Markov basis (Hilbert); it is computable by
elimination; minimal generating sets are unique ``up to minor combinatorial modifications'' [SS03]; the moves in
\emph{every} Markov basis are the indispensable ones (Takemura--Aoki [TA04]). The theorem certifies
connectivity of every fiber at once and says \emph{nothing about rates}: the connectivity/mixing
decoupling is built in from the start.

\textbf{The basis hierarchy: lattice $\subset$ Markov $\subset$ Gr\"obner $\subset$ Graver}
([ALP23, \S3.3]; [Stu96]):
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Lattice basis}: spans every fiber difference over $\mathbb{Z}$ but in general
\textbf{does not connect} --- the combination may be forced through negative territory (minimal
example: $A=(1\,1\,1)$, basis $\{(1,-2,1),(1,-1,0)\}$ leaves $e_3$ isolated on $\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}$).
[DS98] flag published analyses whose ``error consists in assuming that a lattice basis connects
staying positive.'' Spanning-without-connecting is the template for a menu complete in principle but
disconnecting in the constrained space --- the worry transported to the banded ASE walk.
\item \emph{Markov basis}: irreducibility of all fibers; no rate, and \textbf{no} robustness to
added constraints.
\item \emph{Gr\"obner basis}: additionally gives \textbf{directed monotone paths} to the unique
$\prec$-minimal normal form (division algorithm); cost-compatible orders make it the IP optimum
(Conti--Traverso [CT91]). The commutative formalization of oriented rewriting to a canonical form ---
i.e.\ of the [FL25] proof and of \code{fcompress}; its unoriented randomization is the Markov-basis walk.
\item \emph{Graver basis} (conformally-minimal kernel vectors; contains all of the above): the
correct notion for \textbf{constrained} spaces --- bound-respecting Graver moves connect every
\emph{bounded} fiber ([ALP23, Prop.~3.3]; [Sul21, Thm.~9.4.5]), which a Markov basis does
\textbf{not} guarantee; simultaneously a universal optimality certificate [Grav75].
\end{itemize}
\emph{ASE reading:} the band-restricted walk lives on a bounded fiber; the connectivity-correct menu
is the \emph{Graver analog} (moves whose intermediates stay legal), of which a curated DB is an
empirical proxy. ``Complete for the unrestricted fiber, stuck on the banded fiber'' is a
theorem-level phenomenon commutatively, not an implementation bug.

\textbf{Finiteness within a model, unboundedness across families.} With \emph{one} growing direction
(higher Lawrence liftings, e.g.\ $p\times q\times r$ tables, $r\to\infty$), a bounded menu of move
\emph{shapes} serves the whole family (Markov complexity [SS03, Thm.~1]; Graver complexity [HS07]).
With \emph{two} growing directions the opposite: any prescribed vector appears inside \emph{every}
Markov basis of $I\times J\times 3$ no-three-way tables for suitable $I,J$ [DLO06a] --- minimal moves
of unbounded degree and support --- and this family is universal for all rational fiber polytopes
[DLO06b]. The circuit family grows in two directions ($2N$ and $m$); [FL25]-style completeness (via
unbounded intermediate resources) is a one-direction-type result, and the commutative default is that
fixed menus eventually fail on the banded question.

\textbf{Connectivity is not sampling; deficiency $=$ conserved statistics.}
(1) Even solved cases are diffusive with terrible constants (order diameter$^2$, constants
$(D/4)^{D/4}$ in the format [DS98, \S2.3]).
(2) Acceptance statistics do not certify coverage: rejection rates measure local applicability, not
whether the communicating class is the whole fiber [ALP23]; \emph{our conversion-rate curves (\S3.4)
inherit the same blind spot}.
(3) A deficient menu conserves extra statistics in two layers: \emph{lattice deficiency}
($\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{M}\subsetneq\ker_{\mathbb{Z}}A$ --- a linear/torsion invariant,
detectable by Smith normal form before any sampling) and \emph{boundary deficiency} (full span,
disconnected fiber graph --- algebraically a saturation gap
$(I_{\mathcal{M}}:(x_1\cdots x_n)^\infty)=I_A$ [HoSt95], quantified by the relaxation depth $q$
[ALP23, Prop.~4.2], with no universal bound on $q$ [ALP23, Thm.~4.3]). A deficient walk is not slow
--- it \emph{exactly samples a smaller model}. The practical discriminator is the \textbf{relaxation
probe}: temporarily legalize mildly illegal states (ASE analog: widen the band or admit short off-DB
bridge words) and watch whether frozen statistics move --- runnable \emph{without} a fresh
equilibrium sample.
(4) The non-walk alternative: sequential importance sampling of the fiber [CDHL05] --- unbiased, no
mixing time, but the variance can be \emph{silently} exponential (the estimator looks converged and
is exponentially wrong [BSSV06]); the warning that must travel with any circuit-side SIS route to the
missing ``fresh fiber sample.''

\subsection{The transport to the circuit fiber: the dictionary}\label{ss:transport}
Several claims elsewhere (notably \S\ref{ss:stat}'s uniform measure and \S\ref{ss:mix}'s citations)
silently assume parts of the commutative package that do \textbf{not} survive. Below: verified
theorems, code-level facts about \code{fmix} (working tree 2026-08-24), or marked inference.

\textbf{The implemented chain.} One DB move (\code{src/engine/mix.rs:5871--6005, 6204ff};
\code{src/db\_mixing/db\_replace.rs:911--1025}): (1) draw a position, build a window --- \emph{maximal} when the largest-first descent is armed (the
default; with \code{--no-db-prefixes} the outgoing length is uniform in $[1,s_{db}]$, single lookup;
the shipped GSS phase-A profile disables the descent on the MIX channel, \code{prefixes\_mix=false},
and all band/hold arms ran \code{--no-db-prefixes}); (2) when armed, the
\emph{first-hit descent} shrinks the window, canonicalizing and looking up each prefix (litter/span/
degree guards); (3) on a hit, draw \emph{uniformly among mode-eligible candidates} of the canonical
class --- and \emph{if any curated candidate exists, eligibility is restricted to curated ones}; (4)
apply unconditionally after verification. No Metropolis--Hastings correction anywhere; ledger and
size profile are a \emph{feedback controller}, not a stationarity correction.

\textbf{What breaks.} \emph{(i) No module structure:} a DS path is a multiset of moves; an ASE
substitution at position $i$ contributes $w_{<i}rw_{<i}^{-1}$ --- position- and state-dependent
conjugation with no commutative counterpart. Derivations are van Kampen diagrams; ``fiber diameter''
transports to the \emph{Dehn function} of the presentation relative to the band (open).
\emph{(ii) Applicability is word-local and binding:} measured: 2.4--4.1\% of \emph{attempts}
convert (band arms; no descent), 53.7\% of DB \emph{rounds} splice in early phase A (short
descent-free MIX windows $\sim$99\% at length 1--2, plus the curated store; COMP descent rounds land
43.9\%, a COMP round spending up to 12 attempts); Windisch's rejection-free negative
[Win16, Rem.~3.27] transports as: hit-rate levers change the clock, not the per-conversion transport --- the \S3.4
block-size finding, \emph{predicted}. \emph{(iii) Banded connectivity open:} [FL25]'s certificate
routes through a canonical form with no bound on intermediate length, so it does not establish
connectivity of the length-banded fiber (cf.\ lattice-vs-Markov, and [DLO06a]: no universal
bounded-window menu). \emph{(iv) The implemented chain is not reversible:} four code-level proposal
asymmetries (first-hit descent context; length-filter pools $\{\ell-1,\ell,\ell+1\}$ shifting with
$\ell$; curated restriction as a lexicographic prior; no Hastings correction). Which sources are armed is
per-configuration (band/hold arms: no descent, absolute \code{FMIX\_DB\_LEN\_BAND} filter --- their
asymmetry is pool-normalization + curated restriction + history-dependent litter ban + no Hastings;
stable/default runs add the descent context and shifting pools); non-reversibility holds in every
configuration. Its invariant law on
the band is a \textbf{store- and policy-weighted measure of unknown density}, not uniform; the
expansion leg is explicitly driven (splice census: out 1--3 / in 6--13 is controller policy). A
Hastings factor $\min\{1, Q(w'\to w)/Q(w\to w')\}$ would restore a known target (computable in
principle; recorded as a design option). Every theorem in \S\ref{ss:stat} about the uniform measure
describes the target of a reversible chain; ours is not one.

\textbf{What survives.} \emph{(i) Connectivity, classically framed:} [FL25] is the
fundamental-theorem object (a finite menu presenting $\ker(\mathrm{eval})$); substitution histories
are identity sequences [BH82]; Tietze's theorem [LS77] is the analog of ``all Markov bases connect
through $I_A$'': menu choice immaterial for connectivity, decisive (empirically) for rate.
\emph{(ii) The deficiency test, and the red team correctly read:} the zero $x\leftrightarrow y$
affine leak is a statistic of $\mathrm{eval}$ --- constant on the whole fiber, uninformative about
the menu; the 327 interior cross-cut relations are cut-placement-dependent \emph{state} properties,
never tested for conservation along orbits --- they neither confirm nor refute deficiency; $D/L
\approx 3.6$ is an ensemble constant, not an orbit invariant. \textbf{No exact
non-$\mathrm{eval}$ conservation law has been exhibited}: dynamical slowness remains the standing
explanation --- the Windisch scenario. \emph{(iii) The abelianization is an honest DS walk:} census
step-vectors $m_{u,v}=\mathrm{ab}(v)-\mathrm{ab}(u)$ span a menu-lattice $L$ whose conserved
statistics are exactly $L^\perp$ --- computable by exact RREF over the frozen store ($G\le\mathrm{Alt}$
kills the sign character; length is deliberately non-conserved). If $L^\perp=0$: the abelian shadow
carries no conservation law, and every obstruction lives in the non-abelian transport. [Audit not yet
run --- proposed.] \emph{(iv) Block-Gibbs structure:} a DB conversion is block dynamics with bounded
blocks --- but heat-bath w.r.t.\ the \emph{store measure} on spellings, not the conditional uniform.
The dichotomy transports as predictions: bounded blocks $\Rightarrow$ Windisch bottlenecks persist;
unbounded coherent blocks $\Rightarrow$ expansion [SW18]; unrestricted pair heat-bath $\Rightarrow$
$\Theta(n\log n)$ [PS17, Smi14]. The ``line'' analog --- conditional uniform sampling of a whole
segment given its boundary permutation --- is itself the open hard problem: the escape route is real
but self-referential.

\textbf{fcompress as the oriented menu.} Unoriented random application of the catalogue = the ASE
mixer; size-oriented greedy application to termination = \code{fcompress}, a string rewriting system
aimed at normal forms. Squier's theorem [Squ87] is the obstruction theory (finite \emph{convergent}
systems are strictly stronger than finite complete menus; critical-pair 2-cells = non-abelian
syzygies). The compression residuals (94.7\% A1 bench; 96.5\% delivered run\_c) are a distance-to-normal-form
proxy under a non-confluent greedy orientation; the 64.6\% blocked-pair census is a concrete critical-pair
obstruction; [FL25]'s canonical form existing while compression stalls at $\sim$95\% is the
confluence gap made quantitative.

\subsection{The stationary distribution}\label{ss:stat}
If the move set connects the (banded) fiber, the walk is ergodic with a unique stationary law.
\emph{Per \S\ref{ss:transport}.(iv), for the implemented chain that law is a store- and
policy-weighted measure on the banded fiber, not the uniform measure} (a Hastings-corrected variant
would target uniform); the rigidity results below describe the \textbf{uniform} measure and are a
qualitative guide, exact for the corrected chain. The subtle point, forced by the literature: this
equilibrium measure is itself \textbf{rigid}, not a structureless cloud.
\begin{itemize}
\item For the one understood non-commutative word-fiber --- reduced words of the longest element of
$S_n$ (sorting networks) --- the uniform measure is macroscopically deterministic (sine trajectories,
sphere concentration) [AHRV07, Dau22]. \textbf{A persistent skeleton can therefore be an equilibrium
property, not evidence of non-mixing}; to claim the walk is stuck one must compare against a fresh
uniform fiber sample.
\item Near-minimal fibers equilibrate to a rigid frame decorated by Brownian-excursion slack [FLT21]
--- the shape a mixed ASE configuration should take: geodesic skeleton $+$ slack.
\end{itemize}
Hence the target is \emph{(a reweighting of) the measure on the length-banded fiber whose typical
element is a geodesic skeleton plus slack}. A prefix ridge at $\rho=1$ is expected \emph{even at
equilibrium}; the only equilibrium knob on how far the trajectory sits from the original is the
\textbf{slack} $s=m'-|C|$.

\subsection{Why the excursions are taut}\label{ss:taut}
In nonamenable/hyperbolic geometry a walk conditioned on its endpoints stays in a thin corridor of the
geodesic. Kesten [Kes59]: nonamenability $\Leftrightarrow$ exponential return decay. Local limit theorem
$p_n(e,e)\sim C R^{-n} n^{-3/2}$ [Gou14, GL13]: loop mass on the shortest loops, universal excursion
exponent. Deviation inequalities [MS20]: $\Pr[\text{stray}\ge D]\le Ce^{-cD}$. Bridge diffusivity
[BJ99, BIK07, CM17]: a length-$\ell$ bridge reaches depth only $\sim\sqrt\ell$, range first-order below
the free walk; even the unconditioned walk tracks the geodesic at $O(\log n)$ width [Sis17]. The renewal/pinning theory aggregates these: contacts form a renewal process, and in the
localized phase --- where hard-return substitution sits deep --- excursion lengths have exponential
tails, the max excursion is $O(\log m)$, and the path stays in an $O(1)$ tube forever
[Fis84, Gia07, denH09, IY01, CGZ06].

\subsection{Mixing time: the fiber-walk rate theory in detail}\label{ss:mix}
Connectivity is settled ([FL25]; long Hurwitz fibers are a single orbit up to lifting invariants
[CP-in-FV91, EVW16], so persistence must be \emph{dynamical}, not conservation). The \emph{rate} is
where the commutative theory has real content. ([Win16] and [SW18] re-verified at full-text level;
contingency-table results via the survey portion of [CDGJM06].)

\textbf{Two scaling regimes.} Commutative fibers of fixed $A$ have polynomially many points under
dilation (Ehrhart); $|\Phi_C|=\exp(\Theta(m))$. Hence: \emph{dilation regime} ($b\to Nb$; ``rapid''
$=$ polylog$(N)$ --- Windisch's home) vs \emph{system-size regime} ($m\to\infty$; ``rapid'' $=$
poly$(m)$ --- ours). A theorem forbidding polylog mixing under dilation is compatible with diffusive
poly$(N)$ mixing there, and says nothing directly about the system-size regime.

\textbf{The negative pole [Win16], exactly.} For any fixed Markov basis and any dominated sequence
(pure dilation $b_i=ib$ is the archetype), the fiber-graph family is \textbf{no expander}; with
polynomially many points, \textbf{not rapidly mixing}. Quantitatively
\[
  h\big(\mathcal{F}_{A,b_i}(\mathcal{M})\big) \;=\; O(1/i),
\]
with the witness bottleneck a translated sub-fiber whose move-boundary is Ehrhart-codimension-one ---
an obstruction living at the \textbf{positivity boundary} (corners), not in the bulk. Companions:
finite menu enlargement --- up to the \emph{Graver} basis --- has identical asymptotics
(Rem.~3.24); the \emph{rejection-free} walk on applicable moves is also not rapidly mixing
(Rem.~3.27, via the loop-removal comparison Prop.~3.26) --- the rigorous twin of ``the whole
block-size effect is the hit rate'' (\S3.4);
per-fiber \emph{adapted} menus give an expander family (Cor.~4.3) --- the ASE analog would be a menu
whose window inventory grows with the instance. \emph{Transfer caveats:} (1) only
polylog-in-fiber-size mixing is forbidden --- $h = O(1/\mathrm{diam})$ is exactly diffusive behavior,
so no exponential trapping is proved; (2) the boundary-free version (tables over
$\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$) has clean diffusive \emph{cutoff} [NN20] --- the obstruction is a corner
effect, and the ASE analog of the corner is the band and admissibility constraints, a design surface
we control; (3) nothing commutative rules out (or supports) an additional non-abelian slowdown.

\textbf{The positive pole [SW18], exactly.} With move-\emph{lines} as single steps (heat-bath
resampling the whole ray), the compressed fiber-graph diameter is bounded by a constant uniformly
over all fibers of a fixed matrix (Thm.~3.15; unit moves: linear growth), and along dilations of a
fixed polytope the heat-bath walk has a uniform spectral gap (Cor.~5.11) --- the exact dichotomy
partner of [Win16]. \emph{Caution:} the proved gap degrades super-exponentially in the augmentation
length (dimension-like), so ``expander \emph{in fixed dimension}'' is exactly what is proved; in the
ASE transport ``dimension'' scales like $m/w$, so the rigorous positive pole does \textbf{not}
license poly$(m)$ mixing from full-window resampling. Growing-dimension positives exist but are
continuous: Kac's walk $\Theta(n\log n)$ [PS17]; the simplex Gibbs sampler $\Theta(n\log n)$, with a
boundary [Smi14] --- so no single ingredient (conservation, dimension, boundary) is fatal; the
negatives need \emph{bounded increments + lattice geometry + growing domain} together. \emph{ASE
reading:} a DB substitution is a heat-bath draw on one window's fiber \emph{truncated} to the curated
band --- and the ledger below shows truncation can cost an exponential factor at identical footprint.

$2\times2$ \textbf{heat-bath}, $m{=}2$ & window resample & poly(cols, $\log N$) [DGh00] & \textbf{exp.\ speedup at same footprint} \\
$2\times2$ heat-bath, constant $m$ & window resample & rapid [CDGJM06]: flow + [DS93] comparison & positive, fixed rows \\
large margins, continuous + rounding & (not lattice) & poly [DKM97, Mor02] & leave-the-lattice \\
DG over $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ (\textbf{no boundary}) & unit mod $q$ & cutoff at $\frac{n^2\log n}{4(1-\cos(2\pi/q))}$ [NN20] & clean diffusion, no bottleneck \\
lozenge tilings, single flip & local flips & $O(L^4\log L)$ tight [Wil04] & diffusive per site \\
adjacent-transposition shuffle & adjacent swaps & cutoff $\frac{N^3\log N}{2\pi^2}$ single moves [Lac16] & system-size, $\Theta(N^3\log N)$ \\
pinned DGFF, $n$-box in $\mathbb{Z}^2$ & site resample & cutoff $\frac{2}{\pi^2}n^2\log n$ [GG21] & diffusive zero-mode \\
Kawasaki lattice gas, side $L$ & conserved exchange & gap $\asymp L^{-2}$ [LY93] & conservative law \\
matroid bases exchange & single exchange & expansion $\ge1$ [ALOV19]; MLSI $\ge1/r$ [CGM19] & log-concavity does it \\
Coxeter tournaments & local reversals & rapid [BKMP24] & product-structured \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Reading: every \textbf{positive} enlarges a move to a full conditional window resample, fixes a
dimension, removes the boundary, or has log-concavity; every \textbf{negative/slow} has bounded
increments against a growing domain, between diffusive and boundary-bottlenecked. \textbf{Nothing
commutative exhibits worse-than-polynomial mixing for a connecting basis on dilated fibers}: the
rigorous precedent for skeleton persistence is diffusive-type slowdown plus vanishing boundary
conductance, \emph{not} exponential localization (those precedents are the non-fiber results of
\S\ref{ss:taut}).

\textbf{Comparison, decomposition, window equivalence.} (i) Path/congestion comparison [DS93]
transfers gaps between menus (the [CDGJM06] engine). \emph{Bounded-window equivalence}
[derived here, conditional]: \emph{if} in-window derivations have length $O(w)$ and stay in-band
(not supplied by [FL25], whose certificate is unbounded), the simulation congestion is $\exp(O(w))$
and bounded-window menus share a complexity class, with only $w=\Omega(\log m)$ able to change it;
unconditionally the congestion is $\exp(O(L(w)))$ for the unknown in-window derivation length
$L(w)$ --- read the threshold as heuristic. (ii) Decomposition [MR02; JSTV04, Thm.~1]: with skeleton statistic
$\sigma$, $\lambda = \min\{\bar\lambda/3,\ \bar\lambda\lambda_{\min}/(3\gamma+\bar\lambda)\}$. Slow
direction: the Rayleigh quotient converts measurements into an \emph{empirical gap upper bound}
(per-move Dirichlet energy of the aligned-offset profile from move logs --- proposed measurement).
Fast direction: decomposition turns ``the zero-mode is the obstruction'' into a two-part proof
obligation --- (a) the projection chain mixes (open transport problem), (b) slack equilibrates at
fixed skeleton (looks tractable). (iii) Spectral independence [ALO20]: the modern local-mixing
criterion is correlation decay of the stationary measure itself; the ASE influence object is
window--window covariance at word distance $\ell$ --- the measured slack autocorrelation length
(500--750 gates, \S3.3) is the first empirical look at exactly this matrix.

\textbf{Generic floors.} Bottleneck: $t_{\mathrm{mix}}\ge 1/(4\Phi^*)$; with $h=O(1/N)$, linear in
the dilation from conductance alone. Diameter: $t_{\mathrm{mix}}\ge\mathrm{diam}/2$; for reduced
words of $w_0$ the diameter is \emph{exactly} $\frac{1}{24}n(n-1)(n-2)(3n-5)=\Theta(n^4)$ [RR13]
(McConville fix) --- an $\Omega(n^4)$ floor. Varopoulos--Carne [CV85]:
$P^t(x,y)\le 2\sqrt{\pi(y)/\pi(x)}\,e^{-d^2/2t}$, whence $t_{\mathrm{mix}}\ge r^2/(2\ln 8|\Omega|)$
--- distance grows at most $\sqrt{t\log|\Omega|}$; for $w_0$ words, conditionally
$\Omega(n^6/\log n)$ [derived here].

\textbf{Synthesis.} (1) Fixed basis + dilation: conductance $O(1/N)$, proven quadratic in solved
cases, boundary-free cutoff [NN20] --- diffusive, no super-polynomial trapping known. (2) Fixed
window scale + growing system: $t_{\mathrm{mix}} \asymp$ (update sites) $\times$ (transport
diameter)$^2$ $\times$ polylog [LY93, GG21, Lac16, Wil04]. (3) \emph{Transported ASE expectation
[derived here, heuristic]:} skeleton relaxation in $\sim(m/w)^2$polylog sweeps $\approx m^3/w^3$
substitutions --- \emph{provided} the non-abelian charge transports diffusively and the band creates
no Windisch corners (both open). At production scale ($m\sim5\times10^6$, $w\sim12$--$16$):
$\sim10^{16\text{--}17}$ substitutions to the diffusive horizon, versus the $\sim$5--20 conversions
per position ($\sim10^{6\text{--}7}$ total) applied --- nine to ten orders inside the transient; the observed ``fast decay to a floor,
then plateau'' (\S2, pt.~4) is exactly the transient of the diffusive picture; the free-analog
complexity results [OKHH24, BS18, HFKEY22] put full equilibrium further out still. (4) What would
beat the diffusive floor, in decreasing verified strength: full-coset resampling ([SW18]; exponential
speedup at identical footprint [DGh00] --- but no dimension-uniform gap); instance-adapted menus
[Win16, Cor.~4.3]; structural measure properties (log-concavity / spectral independence [ALOV19,
CGM19, ALO20]) --- unavailable until exhibited for fiber word measures; leaving the lattice
[DKM97, Mor02] --- no clear ASE analog.

\textbf{Net.} Local window statistics equilibrate fast; the pinned skeleton is a conserved-density
zero-mode relaxing diffusively at best; each excursion is exponentially confined. Every commutative
tool transports as an inequality \emph{template}; none of the matching upper bounds transport (they
lean on abelian Fourier structure, log-concavity, or fixed dimension). The precise mixing time of
relator-substitution walks with fixed product in a non-abelian group remains, per our survey,
\textbf{open} (full annotations: \code{docs/PINNED\_WALK\_LITERATURE.md}, 84 works).

\section{Evidence that DB-based mixing is an ASE process}
The DB-substitution mixer is an ASE process by construction, and every signature checks out:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Fiber walk by construction.} Each move swaps a window for a DB-equivalent; the global
permutation is invariant (corner cells $H(0,0)=H(m,m')=0$ in every plate). Which \emph{measure} on
$\Phi_C$ it targets is store/controller design, not detailed balance (\S\ref{ss:transport}).
\item \textbf{Trajectory = short returning bridges.} The production splice census
[phasea-splice-census]: outgoing 1--3 gates, incoming 6--13, $53.7\%$ splice rate, $38.6\%$ diagonal.
\item \textbf{Progress diagonal survives ($\rho\approx1$).} 22 band arms and the pipeline: $\rho=0.998$--%
$1.000$ in $\sim$180 affine plates; raw-HD ridge $\sim7\sigma$/row end-to-end in run\_c/run\_d --- the
\S\ref{ss:stat}/\S\ref{ss:taut} prediction, not a failure to move.
\item \textbf{Ridge decays, front-loads, saturates} (Fig.~G4): fast loss then plateau, contrast flat,
$\rho$ unbroken --- diffusive zero-mode relaxation [LY93, GG21].
\item \textbf{Quality per conversion is invariant} (Fig.~G3): decay vs conversions collapses every
rule and band onto one curve --- the \S\ref{ss:mix} statement.
\item \textbf{Displacement is transversely localized, compact-tailed} (\S3): tight $\Di$, knife-edge
valley --- localized-phase/taut-bridge signature.
\item \textbf{No interior state collision.} Hamming floor $=0$ only at endpoints; interior minima
\emph{rise} with mixing --- skeleton $+$ bounded slack.
\end{enumerate}
The \S\ref{ss:stat} caveat (persistence could be equilibrium rigidity) is consistent with, not against,
the data: we are transient (point 4, [OKHH24]), so persistence is both equilibrium-compatible and
incomplete relaxation; separating them needs a fresh fiber sample we cannot draw at $N{=}128$ ---
though the \emph{relaxation probe} of \S\ref{ss:markov} (widen the band / admit short off-DB bridge
words, watch whether frozen statistics move) is an intervention-based discriminator that needs no
fresh sample.

\subsection{Calibrating the instruments (Fig.~G1)}
Random $D$-gate circuits, $D\in\{12,25,50,100,200,400,800\}$, 50 reps, four ensembles per width
$N\in\{128,64\}$: \emph{cens} (measured census = the ruler), \emph{aff} (pure NOT/CNOT), \emph{nl}
(pure $c\ge2$), \emph{seg} (real mixed segments). Two exact laws:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Hamming = Ehrenfest.} All gates are comp=1, 2-control (fire prob $3/4$):
$H(D)=\tfrac n2(1-e^{-sD/(n/2)})$, $n$ = substrate wire count (raw $n$-wire arms, not the $2N$
gadget convention), $s=0.751\pm0.010$ ($n{=}128$), $0.769\pm0.015$ ($n{=}64$),
reduced $\chi^2=0.33$. Inversion $D=-\tfrac{n}{2s}\ln(1-H/\tfrac n2)$.

$0.5-H_{\mathrm{aff}}(D)=0.5\,e^{-D/\kappa}$, $\kappa=128.65\pm1.22$ ($N{=}128$), $64.09\pm0.94$
($N{=}64$); $\kappa/n=1.005,1.001$; no lag phase. Pure-affine reads exactly $0.0000$ at every length.
\item $\triangle$ \textbf{seg is not a rescaled random circuit}: multi-rate, suppressed asymptotes (HD
tail $44/64$ at $N{=}128$), so ``nominal leftover gates'' is model-dependent; the census ruler
(``effective incompressible gates'') is the well-defined unit.
\end{itemize}

\section{Effective $\Di$ for the main pipeline (run\_c / run\_d)}
The production pipeline differs from the band substrate in three ways: full scale (millions of gates),
single-carrier GSS encoding on $2N{=}512$ wires, and the structural phases (split/crossing/fcompress)
on top of DB mixing. The adjacent session (2026-08-23) measured its displacement with the \textbf{raw
Hamming} instrument on the delivered nocomp runs run\_c/run\_d ($|C'|$: run\_c $693{,}377\to$ phaseA
$1{,}410{,}438\to$ final $4{,}859{,}911$ gates). Tools: \code{reports/plot\_hmap\_slice.py},
\code{reports/hmap\_aligned\_profile.py}.

\subsection{Phase A only --- a razor-sharp aligned dip (Fig.~G8--G10, Table~T3)}
A textbook ASE prefix ridge: sharp diagonal ($\rho\approx0.99$, perm $z\approx24$), 99--100\% of
interior rows detectable. Halfway-row dip $5.5/5.3$ bits below the 256-bit random-pair baseline (of
$2N{=}512$ wires), core FWHM 2{,}000--2{,}500 gates ($0.15$--$0.18\%$ of $|C'|$), centered within
$0.5\%$; per-row mean $|$peak$|$ 4.56/5.65 bits, \textbf{100\% sign-correlated} (all dips). Through the
Ehrenfest ruler at $n_w{=}512$ wires: a residual deviation $\Delta$ below the 256-bit plateau inverts
as $D=-\tfrac{n_w}{2s}\ln(\Delta/\tfrac{n_w}{2})$ --- for the halfway dip ($\Delta=5.5$) this is
$D\approx1.3$k effective gates; the validated reader (\code{reports/hd\_depth\_to\_displacement.py},
[bandscale-50m]) reads run\_c gss$\to$phaseA at $D/n_w=2.79$, and the certified bound agrees in order
($\Di\ge H\approx250$ bits $\Rightarrow$ $\ge250$ gates). An earlier revision read the dip through
the \emph{linear} branch ($\approx7$--$8$ gates); at saturation ($256-5.5=250.5$ bits) that is the
wrong branch --- near the plateau each displaced gate shrinks the deviation by only
$\sim s/(n_w/2)\approx0.3\%$, so 5.5 residual bits is what a $\sim$1.3k-gate displacement looks like.
The qualitative reading survives corrected: by raw HD the aligned pair is only 5.5 bits more
distinguishable than a random pair --- a sharp, aligned, all-dip diagonal on a saturated background.

\subsection{Full pipeline --- the ridge survives but changes character (Fig.~G8--G10, Table~T3)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{86\% of interior rows still detectable} --- position along the computation is recoverable
end-to-end (raw-HD ridge $\sim7\sigma$/row).
\item \textbf{The sign flips row to row}: run\_c 65/35 dip/bump, run\_d 51/49; the halfway row is an
\emph{anti-correlated bump} ($+3.5/+4.1$ bits, $H>256$).
$\triangle$ \textbf{Read these plates by $|H-256|$, not $\min H$} --- the naive ridge stat
($\rho=0.64$, ``no halfway dip'') misses the anti-correlated half. The flips are affine-complement
re-encodings (split/crossing NOT-absorption).
\item \textbf{Broader, wandering ridge}: mean $|$peak$|$ 2.9/2.95 bits, core FWHM 9.6--19.2k gates
($0.2$--$0.4\%$), location wander IQR $\pm40$--60k gates ($\sim\pm1\%$).

\subsection{Conclusion on effective $\Di$ for the pipeline}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Per-position displacement is $\approx1.3$--$1.6$k effective gates: $D/n_w=2.79$
(phaseA) and $3.09$ (final)} (validated reader \code{reports/hd\_depth\_to\_displacement.py}) ---
about $6\times$ the band-experiment steady state $D/n_w\approx0.5$ (median 64 at $n_w{=}128$), and
\emph{off the end of the band curve}: phase A got there on 5.6 conversions/gate (band reference: 19.4)
but with 19.1M long-range twist relocations the band arms lack --- move type, not dose. The raw-HD
deviation is only 3--5.5 bits not because displacement is small but because deviation decays
exponentially in $D$. The HD-only readout cannot resolve the nonlinear component the affine instrument
exposed ($D_{\mathrm{aff}}/D_{\mathrm{ham}}=1.26$); the affine plate of the pipeline is the missing
measurement.
\item \textbf{The structural phases, not more DB mixing, change the ridge's character} --- broadening
it 3--5$\times$, adding $\pm1\%$ wander, flipping its sign on $\sim$half the rows. This matches
\S\ref{ss:mix}: DB mixing alone relaxes displacement diffusively toward a small floor.
\item \textbf{Position remains recoverable end-to-end} (86\%, $\sim7\sigma$): the progress diagonal
is a conserved zero-mode local mixing shallows but does not erase --- and good local statistics would
not contradict this ($k$-wise independence can coexist with detectable global structure [DPT26]).
Displacement is already $\approx3\,n_w$; the ridge survives because deviation decays exponentially in
$D$, and invisibility at the current 512-sample noise floor needs $D/n_w\approx4.2$ --- another
$\approx1.3$--$1.5\times$ in $D$ at every cut. The binding resource is the \emph{conversion of slack
into composing, non-cancelling displacement} --- empirically the long-range twist channel, not DB
dose --- not slack (ample) or move count (fixed yield per conversion).
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Open measurement.} The most informative next step is an affine (LP) prefix plate of the
run\_c/run\_d final, read by the nonlinear ruler of \S3.1: it would convert the pipeline's ridge into
an effective-gate $\Di$ directly comparable to Table~T1 and resolve whether the sign-flip structure
carries nonlinear displacement or is purely affine bookkeeping.

\section{References}
Annotations state what each work predicts about the ASE process. Full 84-work bibliography:
\code{docs/PINNED\_WALK\_LITERATURE.md}.

\textbf{Fiber walks / Markov bases.}
\textbf{[DS98]} Diaconis \& Sturmfels, \emph{Algebraic algorithms for sampling from conditional
distributions}, Ann.\ Stat.\ 26(1), 1998 --- founding fiber-walk paper; commutative template
(``insert a relator from a finite basis''); decouples connectivity from mixing.
\textbf{[Win16]} Windisch, \emph{Rapid mixing and Markov bases}, SIAM J.\ Discrete Math.\ 30(4), 2016
(arXiv:1505.03018) --- for any fixed Markov basis, dilated fibers are no expander family: edge expansion
$O(1/i)$ (Thm.~1.1); neither finite enlargement (to Graver, Rem.~3.24) nor rejection-free sampling
(Rem.~3.27) helps; adapted menus do (Cor.~4.3). Forbids polylog-in-fiber-size mixing only (consistent
with diffusive); the bottleneck is a positivity-boundary corner effect. Closest rigorous analog of
skeleton persistence.
\textbf{[SW18]} Stanley \& Windisch, \emph{Heat-bath random walks with Markov bases}, 2018
(arXiv:1605.08386) --- line moves give constant fiber diameter uniformly (Thm.~3.15) and an expander
family along dilations \emph{in fixed dimension} (Cor.~5.11; gap degrades super-exponentially in the
augmentation length). The lever is window length --- with no dimension-uniform guarantee.
\textbf{[FL25]} Feng \& Li, \emph{A complete set of transformation rules for reversible circuits}, 2025
(arXiv:2508.17273) --- the literal Markov basis for $\Phi_C$: five complete local rules $\Rightarrow$
connected. Says nothing about rate.
\textbf{[AHRV07]} Angel, Holroyd, Romik \& Vir\'ag, \emph{Random sorting networks}, Adv.\ Math.\
215(2), 2007 --- uniform measure on a non-commutative word-fiber is macroscopically rigid; a persistent
skeleton can be equilibrium, not non-mixing.
\textbf{[Dau22]} Dauvergne, \emph{The Archimedean limit of random sorting networks}, JAMS 2022
(arXiv:1802.08934) --- proves AHRV rigidity; null model for ``fully mixed.''
\textbf{[FLT21]} F\'eray, Louf \& Th\'evenin, \emph{fixed-genus factorizations into transpositions},
2021 (arXiv:2105.03284) --- near-minimal fiber equilibrium = rigid frame $+$ Brownian-excursion slack.
\textbf{[RR13]} Reiner \& Roichman, \emph{Diameter of graphs of reduced words and galleries}, Trans.\ AMS 365,
2013 (arXiv:0906.4768) --- reduced-word flip-graph diameter exactly $\frac{1}{24}n(n-1)(n-2)(3n-5)
= \Theta(n^4)$ in type A (McConville fix): an $\Omega(n^4)$ mixing floor, conditionally
$\Omega(n^6/\log n)$ via Varopoulos--Carne.
\textbf{[ALP23]} Almendra-Hern\'andez, De Loera \& Petrovi\'c, \emph{Markov bases: a 25 year update},
JASA 2024 (arXiv:2306.06270) --- the modern survey: hierarchy containments, Graver connectivity of
bounded fibers, lattice-basis failures, relaxation theory, partial-move-set limitations (the
deficiency test).
\textbf{[SS03]} Santos \& Sturmfels, \emph{Higher Lawrence configurations}, JCTA 103, 2003 --- Markov
complexity: one growing direction $\Rightarrow$ bounded move shapes suffice.
\textbf{[TA04]} Takemura \& Aoki, \emph{Some characterizations of minimal Markov basis \dots},
Ann.\ Inst.\ Statist.\ Math.\ 56(1), 2004 --- minimal-basis structure/uniqueness; indispensable moves
(those in every Markov basis).
