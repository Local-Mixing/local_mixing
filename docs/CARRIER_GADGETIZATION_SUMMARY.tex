\section{Scope and bottom line}

This note consolidates the historical linear and current single-carrier
baselines, the implemented supplied/strong five-, six-, and seven-carrier
value representations, and the aggregate-Gray, no-Gray, micro-Gray,
sentinel-Gray, and post-layout fragmentation spellings of a product fold.  All
constructions are exact reversible circuits and preserve arbitrary incoming
junk on borrowed wires.  The statistical statements below are exhaustive
properties of the small public carrier transition or measurements on one
matched $n=16$ Phase-A fixture.  They are not a cryptographic reduction.

The exact local endpoint-recovery ladder is

\[
\underbrace{1}_{\text{XOR pair and product-share single carrier}}
\longrightarrow
\underbrace{2}_{\text{supplied five}}
\longrightarrow
\underbrace{3}_{\text{strong five and both sixes}}
\longrightarrow
\underbrace{4}_{\text{seven}} .
\]

This is the algebraic degree needed to recover a target gate's firing bit from
its identified before/after carrier tuple.  It is \emph{not} the degree of the
external product mask: the production single-carrier snapshot already has
quadratic and cubic mask terms.  Those time-invariant terms cancel across one
local target update, which is why that older endpoint witness remains affine.

The default remains aggregate Gray because it is compact and Phase-A friendly.
It has, however, an exact affine space--time witness: in single-carrier mode,
subtracting one dirty accumulator before and after the complete gather gives
the complete mask sum; in the representation-aware five/six/seven folds it
gives the complete operand directly.  Thus aggregate Gray collapses internal
trace recovery back to degree one regardless of the carrier endpoint degree.
Four-share micro-Gray removes that one-interval witness and had the best
measured Phase-A rates, but its matched circuit was $4.56\times$ larger.
No-Gray avoids intentional aggregation.  Native post-fragmentation restores
most frozen-store reachability, but still starts $3.74\times$ larger than Gray.
Sentinel Gray is compact and keeps the maximum-degree atom out of every
accumulator, but exposes the lower-degree sum with a very strong sparse
predictor and performed poorly against this frozen store.  None is ``safe
against all practical attacks'' merely by choosing a carrier count or fold.

A new whole-trace audit also finds the high-degree cancellation seen in FIRE:
even no-Gray seven has an exact weight-54 XOR of degree-5--11 states of one
carrier wire that recovers a degree-three source firing, plus a weight-36 XOR
of degree-10/11 states that recovers a degree-ten firing.  Seven therefore
raises the local endpoint tensor boundary but is not a defense against a
size-100 Gaussian search over time-indexed raw wire values.

\section{Three different meanings of degree}

The word ``degree'' was used for several different attack models in the earlier
reports.  They must be kept separate.

Let $x,y\in\{0,1\}^k$ be a target's carrier tuple immediately before and after
one local class update.  Let $F$ be the logical target flip and let
$\delta_\kappa=\kappa^-\xor\kappa^+$ be the public ledger change.  The external
mask $M(B)$ is unchanged across that update, so

\begin{equation}
  F=v^-\xor v^+
   =D(x)\xor D(y)\xor\delta_\kappa .
  \label{eq:endpoint-recovery}
\end{equation}

The known constant $\delta_\kappa$ does not change algebraic degree.  ``Exact
endpoint degree $d$'' below means that $F$ is outside the span of every
square-free monomial of degree below $d$ in the $2k$ endpoint coordinates, and
enters that span at degree $d$.  These claims assume that the attacker has
already identified the tuple and the before/after boundary.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.25\linewidth}rrp{0.31\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
representation & $\deg D$ & full snapshot $\deg(D\xor M)$ & exact local endpoint recovery \\
\midrule
historical XOR pair & 1 & 1 & degree 1: $F=c_0^-+c_1^-+c_0^++c_1^++\delta_\kappa$; normally only one carrier has net change \\
production single carrier, $[2,2,2,3]$ mask & 1 & 3 & degree 1: $F=c^-+c^++\delta_\kappa$ \\
supplied five & 2 & 3 & degree 2 \\
strong cubic five & 3 & 3 & degree 3 \\
compact or structural-strong six & 3 & 3 & degree 3 \\
retained seven & 4 & 4 & degree 4 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Thus the earlier degree-one weakness was \emph{not} that the production
product mask had degree one.  A single-carrier snapshot is

\[
v=c\xor q_1(B)\xor q_2(B)\xor q_3(B)\xor h(B)\xor\kappa,
\qquad \deg q_i=2,\quad\deg h=3,
\]

and is degree three in the current physical carrier/band coordinates.  The
problem is that the same $q_i,h$ occur on both sides of a recognized update and
cancel, leaving $F=c^-\xor c^+\xor\delta_\kappa$.  Supplied five replaces that
affine endpoint relation by a quadratic one; strong five and both six-carrier
maps make it cubic; seven makes it quartic.

The implementation pins these boundaries by exhaustive GF(2) feature-rank
tests, not only by inspecting the displayed ANFs.  Appending $F$ raises rank
when it is outside the tested feature span and leaves rank unchanged when it is
recoverable.  Supplied five changes $11\to12$ at degree one and stays 42 at
degree two; strong five changes $22\to23$ at degree two and stays 42 at degree
three.  Compact six changes $29\to30$ at degree two and admits $F$ at degree
three; structural-strong six changes $53\to54$ at degree two and stays 103 at
degree three.  Seven changes $160\to161$ through degree three and stays 226 at
degree four.

This endpoint degree is also different from \emph{parity-detector weight}.  An
XOR of 20 observed coordinates is still a degree-one polynomial, while an exact
degree-two recovery can be an XOR of many pairwise products.  Consequently the
supplied five map can have zero correlation with every weight-one and
weight-two endpoint parity and still admit the exact quadratic recovery
$F=D_5(x)\xor D_5(y)$.  Likewise, an inexact parity correlation and an exact
tensor-lift recovery answer different questions.

Finally, the old reconstruction heatmaps were global, single-prefix searches
for a progress-aligned state.  A flat degree-one heatmap does not contradict an
exact local relation once a carrier tuple, update boundary, or two internal
prefixes have been aligned.  The next section's degree-one Gray witness is in
this time-unrolled coordinate space, not in the original input variables.

\section{Carrier representations}

Every logical value is $v=D(c_0,\ldots,c_{k-1})\xor M(B)\xor\kappa$.
The external production mask plan is $[2,2,2,3]$ in every current row.  In the
multi-carrier rows, a gate first applies a public class-preserving permutation
$U_0$ to the target tuple and then conditionally flips $c_0$;
$U_1=U_0\xor e_0$.  Both $U_0$ and $U_1$ are fixed-point-free and have the
required decode transition.  The production single-carrier row instead
updates its one carrier directly.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.08\linewidth}p{0.25\linewidth}p{0.14\linewidth}p{0.17\linewidth}p{0.22\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
$k$ & decode/update & physical width & raw endpoint parity & exact endpoint algebra \\
\midrule
1 (production single) & $D_1=c_0$; external $[2,2,2,3]$ product mask & $n+b$; auto $2n$ & perfect weight-2 relation $c^-+c^+$, up to the known ledger change & degree 1 exactly; the degree-2/3 masks cancel across the update \\
5 (supplied) & $D_5=c_0+c_1c_2+c_1c_3+c_2c_3+c_1c_4$; exact 40-gate signed $U_0$ & $5n+b$; auto $6n$ & zero through weight 2; first weight 3 has $|\rho|=.5$ (75\% agreement) & degree 2 exactly: $f=D_5(x)+D_5(y)$.  It is \emph{not} immune to the second tensor. \\
5 (strong cubic) & $D=c_0+c_2+c_2c_3+c_1c_2c_3+c_1c_2c_4+c_3c_4$; six-gate $U_0$ & $5n+b$; auto $6n$ & zero through weight 2; two weight-3 detectors have $|\rho|=.25$ (62.5\% agreement) & outside degree 2, exact at degree 3.  The compact affine tail leaves $c_1,c_2$ fixed, a structural distinguisher. \\
6 & $D_6$ has maximum degree 3; compact 10-gate $U_0$ & $6n+b$; auto $7n$ & zero through weight 3; first weight 4 has $|\rho|=.25$ (62.5\%) & outside degree 2, exact at degree 3.  Two lanes are frozen by the compact update, a structural distinguisher. \\
6 (strong structural) & same cubic $D_6$; nonlinear 21-gate $U_0$ & $6n+b$; auto $7n$ & zero through weight 3; first weight 4 remains $|\rho|=.25$ (62.5\%) & outside degree 2, exact at degree 3; full affine endpoint rank and every lane moves. Some higher-weight coefficients reach $|\rho|=.5$, versus $.25$ for the compact map. \\
7 (best) & $D_7=c_0+c_1+c_2+c_3c_4+c_5c_6+c_3c_4c_5c_6$; nonlinear 26-gate $U_0$ & $7n+b$; auto $8n$ & zero through weight 3; first weight 4 has $|\rho|=.25$ (62.5\%), 21 detectors, three at the maximum & outside degrees 1--3, exact at degree 4; full affine endpoint rank (no perfect affine graph relation). \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Thus nonlinear carriers repair the single-carrier's affine local endpoint
witness without changing the external mask plan.  The strong cubic five
reaches the supplied six's algebraic degree with
fewer wires, but not its weight-3 parity immunity and with stronger affine-tail
structure.  Strong six removes the compact six's frozen/equal-lane affine
relations without changing its first sparse-parity or exact-degree boundary;
it costs 11 extra update gates and is not a higher-weight Walsh dominance.
Six-to-seven raises the exact degree from 3 to 4 but does not improve
the first raw parity's weight or bias.  If an attacker identifies the local
$2k$ before/after tuple, all three tables are tiny; the global-width tensor
count is meaningful only if tuple and update boundaries remain hidden.

A bounded strong-seven search did not produce an honest replacement.  Every
candidate found with lower first weight-four bias ($|\rho|=.125$) admitted an
exact degree-three endpoint recovery.  Among 1,404 completed degree-four
candidates, none improved the shipped map's 21 nonzero weight-four supports
and spectrum energy; the retained alternatives had at least 23.  A 19-gate
candidate was smaller but statistically worse.  The existing
\code{seven-carrier} mode is therefore the strong/Pareto implementation under
the chosen priority, rather than adding a ``strong'' alias for a weaker trade.

\section{High-weight cancellation among high-degree trace features}

There is a fourth attack model beyond raw endpoint parity, bounded-degree
endpoint monomials, and the explicit Gray accumulator witness.  Give every
physical wire state at every gate boundary its own column
$\phi_j:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$.  Ordinary Gaussian elimination asks whether an
underlying source value $T$ is in

\[
  T=a_0+\sum_{j\in J}\phi_j .
\]

The input ANF degree of a column is irrelevant to this elimination.  Fifty
degree-eleven columns can cancel exactly to a degree-three target.  This is
not a degree-two input-monomial attack and it is not contradicted by the local
degree-four endpoint result.  It is an affine attack in the much larger
\emph{time-unrolled trace-feature space}.

The supplied-five update already has an isolated example.  If
$c_i^{(t)}$ is carrier coordinate $i$ after $t$ applications of its public
$U_0$, then

\begin{align*}
D_5={}&c_1^{(0)}+c_2^{(1)}+c_0^{(2)}+c_1^{(2)}+c_3^{(2)}
       +c_1^{(3)}+c_2^{(3)}\\
    &+c_0^{(4)}+c_1^{(4)}+c_4^{(4)}+c_0^{(5)} .
\end{align*}

Ten selected trace coordinates have ANF degree four and one has degree one,
yet their weight-eleven XOR is the quadratic decode.  Meet-in-the-middle
enumeration proves weight eleven is minimum within these first six time
slices.  The full fixed-$U_0$ orbit comparison is:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
map & orbit period & coordinate columns & trace rank & $D$ in span? \\
\midrule
supplied five & 624 & 3,120 & $30/32$ & yes; weight-11 upper bound \\
strong five & 4 & 20 & $8/32$ & no; augmented rank 9 \\
compact six & 2 & 12 & $8/64$ & no; augmented rank 9 \\
strong six & 28 & 168 & $31/64$ & no; augmented rank 32 \\
seven & 41,860 & 293,020 & $56/128$ & no; augmented rank 57 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Thus strong five, both sixes, and seven exactly defeat this \emph{isolated
repeated-$U_0$} cancellation even when their complete orbit is offered.  That
is a real improvement over supplied five, but it does not extend to the full
gadget.  Source-dependent updates, rolls, refills, interactions among values,
and the port machinery add trace columns and new cancellation paths.

An exact matched full-gadget experiment used $n=11$, 132 source gates,
$2^{11}=2048$ exhaustively enumerated honest-slice inputs, and the same source,
sandwich, and seeds for every mode.  Candidate columns were the affine
constant, initial wire states, and physical target deltas; every selected delta
was expanded and telescoped to raw before/after wire checkpoints.  The table
counts exact witnesses of raw support at most 100 for the 96 source firings
whose target ANF degree is at least ten.  ``Carrier'' means that every selected
checkpoint lies in the physical home carrier region; rolling can change its
semantic role during the trace.

\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr rr@{}}
\toprule
& \multicolumn{2}{c}{no Gray} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{aggregate Gray} \\
map & found (carrier) & best weight & found (carrier) & best weight \\
\midrule
single & $9\ (4)$ & 6 & $7\ (0)$ & 63 \\
supplied five & $0\ (0)$ & --- & $19\ (7)$ & 4 \\
strong five & $2\ (2)$ & 40 & $71\ (22)$ & 2 \\

seven & $8\ (6)$ & 36 & $66\ (29)$ & 2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

These are exact equalities, not statistical fits.  In particular, no-Gray
seven has

\[
  F_{53}=1+\bigoplus_{g\in L_{36}}w_{59}^{(g)},
\]

where $F_{53}$ is a degree-ten source firing and the 36 terms are snapshots of
one fixed physical carrier location.  Sixteen selected functions have degree
ten and twenty have degree eleven.  More directly analogous to the observed
degree-three reconstruction, the same no-Gray seven artifact has

\[
  F_{3}=1+\bigoplus_{g\in L_{54}}w_{4}^{(g)} .
\]

$F_3$ has degree three; all 54 selected terms are states of the same physical
carrier wire, and their degrees are

\[
  2\times5, 1\times6, 1\times7, 7\times9,
  15\times10, 28\times11.
\]

Both identities were replayed over all 2,048 inputs.  They are the same
mechanism as a weight-81 XOR of degree-15/16 carrier states: high-degree time
evolution telescopes to an underlying source quantity.  Consequently seven
carriers do \emph{not} thwart size-100 raw-time Gaussian attacks in the current
full circuit.  Seven improves the isolated update and often raises observed
support, but carrier count is not monotonic under this attack.

Adding an eighth or ninth carrier is therefore not a justified response by
itself.  In this matched no-Gray fixture the best observed high-degree firing
weights for strong five, compact six, strong six, and seven were respectively
40, 38, 42, and 36.  Extra lanes raise the local endpoint degree only if the
decode is redesigned, while simultaneously adding observable columns and
physical update gates.  Public rolling and public per-occurrence relabeling
preserve the trace span; they can change witness sparsity but add no hidden
entropy.  A larger $k$ should be accepted only after the \emph{compiled whole
trace}, not merely $D/U_0$, shows increasing minimum dependency support over
several $n$ and seeds.  The higher-priority redesign is to avoid aggregate
gathers, vary the class-switching lane and update map across occurrences, use
a family whose every member passes the complete-orbit test, and synthesize
against whole-trace dependency support.  Even public diversification is a
sparsity optimization rather than a proof; fresh unobserved runtime remasking
would be a qualitatively different defense.

Aggregate Gray is materially worse.  For the strong multi-carrier maps, most
degree-ten firings acquire a found weight-at-most-100 relation, and some
weight-two relations are simply one physical target delta.  Among 98
degree-at-least-ten changed-source-target prefix states, the chronological
basis found no weight-at-most-100 witness for any no-Gray multi-carrier map,
but aggregate Gray found 18, 60, 56, 57, and 61 for supplied five through
seven.  A zero here is not a nonexistence proof; it only says this basis did not
produce such a witness.

The tensor-lift view is also important.  Once an update tuple and its boundary
are known, $F=D(x)+D(y)$ has explicit monomial-column support at most 10, 6,
11, 13, and 12 for supplied five, strong five, compact six, strong six, and
seven respectively.  Seven raises the required feature degree to four, but it
does not make the relation high-support after degree-four columns have been
materialized.

The support search is syndrome decoding: ordinary elimination returns one
basis-dependent witness and does not prove minimum Hamming weight.  The table
therefore supplies definitive attacks where a witness was found, but no
security claim where it reports zero.  Exact witnesses have correlation one;
the separate problem of finding the best imperfect correlation under a
support cap was not solved for the zero cells.  The reusable exhaustive tool is
\code{tools/trace\_xor\_support.py}; it retains provenance, expands deltas to
raw checkpoints, reports target and feature ANF degrees, searches alternate
bases, and replay-validates every emitted equality.

\section{Fold constructions and temporal disclosure}

For the production single-carrier operand write
$S=c+q_1+q_2+q_3+h$, where the $q_i$ are quadratic and $h$ is cubic.

Endpoint degree and time-unrolled trace degree are not interchangeable.  With
wire-at-prefix observations treated as separate variables, the exact known
relations are:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.28\linewidth}p{0.43\linewidth}p{0.21\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
fold & exact time-unrolled relation & consequence \\
\midrule
no Gray & no complete operand is deliberately accumulated & no collapse of the endpoint degree is introduced; no whole-trace theorem \\
aggregate Gray, single carrier & $M=u^-\xor u^+$, hence $S=c\xor u^-\xor u^+\xor\kappa$ & exact degree 1 in three physical-time coordinates \\
aggregate Gray, 5/6/7 carriers & the adjusted fold gathers complete $S=D+M+\kappa$, so $S=u^-\xor u^+$ & exact degree 1 in two internal-time coordinates \\
four-share micro-Gray & $S=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{4}(u_j^-\xor u_j^+)$ & still exact degree 1, but eight coordinates must be identified and recombined \\
sentinel Gray & $Q=u^-\xor u^+$; maximum-degree $H$ is not gathered & no complete one-interval operand; strong partial predictor \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The micro row is therefore a higher-support affine witness, not an increase in
algebraic degree.  This is
why a nonlinear carrier and a non-aggregating fold solve different layers of
the problem.

\begin{description}
\item[Aggregate Gray (mode 1, default).]
One dirty accumulator gathers $M=q_1+q_2+q_3+h$.  The exact witness is
$M=u_{\rm before}+u_{\rm after}$ and $S=c+M+\kappa$, using three
physical-time coordinates.  This is why the old independent-prefix audit was
insufficient.  In each representation-aware five/six/seven implementation the
adjusted gather instead includes all atoms of $D$, every external mask atom,
and the ledger constant.  Its before/after XOR is the complete $S$ directly.
Consequently the degree-3/4 carrier endpoint result does not survive an attacker
who can recognize and bracket this internal aggregate.

\item[Expanded/no-Gray (mode 0).]
The product is expanded atom by atom; the Phase-A preset ladders only widths
through four.  No full operand is intentionally gathered.  The known carrier
predictor has $\rho=(1/2)^3(3/4)=.09375$, but the public ladders still expose
partial products and no whole-trace hardness theorem is claimed.

\item[Four-share micro-Gray (mode 2).]
Partition each formal operand into four nonempty shares.  For every one of the
16 share rectangles, dirty $u,z$ execute
\[P;\ u\mathrel{\xor}=q;\ P;\ z\mathrel{\xor}=r;\ P;
  \ u\mathrel{\xor}=q;\ P;\ z\mathrel{\xor}=r,\qquad P:t\mathrel{\xor}=uz .\]
The rectangle contributes exactly $qr$ and restores every borrowed wire.
Quartics use two arbitrary-dirty helpers and eight width-two primitives.
There is no complete single-interval gather, but the four public share deltas
XOR to the operand (eight time coordinates).  That recombination is still an
exact affine equation; a structure-aware attacker can locate and combine the
shares.  This is support-widening hardening, not a higher-degree proof.

\item[Maximum-degree sentinel Gray (mode 3).]
Partition $S=L+Q+H$, with $L$ the nonempty linear atoms, $H$ every
maximum-degree atom, and $Q$ the intermediate degrees.  Constants are absorbed
into a live linear polarity, never an empty atom.  The four-phase identity
gathers only $Q$, while $H$ remains in explicit cross tails.  Cross-tail
ladders of total width at most four force rung zero to contain one blind/cross
factor and one $H$ literal; wider tails remain fossils.  Four borrows are drawn
from four distinct unrelated logical carrier groups.  Exhaustive tests cover
signed constants and dirty-junk five/six/seven folds.  The isolated accumulator
trace has no one-interval $H$ or full-$S$ gather, but $Q$ is intentionally
visible.  In the measured default single-carrier fixture, $c+Q$ predicts the
production operand at 87.5\% ($\rho=.75$).  The generic expression is $L+Q$;
the quoted bias is not automatically a five/six/seven measurement.  This is a
performance-oriented compromise, not LPN-safe.

\item[Post-layout fragmentation.]
After the commuting shuffle, replace each remaining gate wider than two
by a contiguous cap-two arbitrary-dirty double sweep.  \code{exact} uses plain
conjunctions.  \code{native-deep} keeps rung zero exact and spells target reads
and deeper rungs in g57/CNOT form.  Deeper spelling residuals cancel in the
sweep; an inexact first rung would leave a real error, so that invariant is
tested over widths 3--8, both complement values, mixed polarities, and dirty
helpers.  This improves vocabulary, not temporal security relative to no-Gray.
\end{description}

I also audited, but did not implement, a two-tile four-dimensional hypercube
Gray schedule.  It splits the four nonlinear atoms into balanced tiles
$\{q,q\}$ and $\{q,h\}$, traverses a balanced 16-state Gray cycle, and emits
each tile-pair product on its two-dimensional subcube.  Exhaustive abstract
enumeration confirms exactness for arbitrary dirty baselines.  Its first exact
operand witness has weight 5 (carrier plus both two-snapshot tile deltas); the
best weight-3 predictor has $|\rho|=.375$ (68.75\% agreement).  Estimated
canonical cost is 141 primitives per generic production fold, between sentinel
and four-share micro.  It is a promising next A/B arm, but remains structurally
recombinable and has no frozen-store measurement, so mode 4 was deliberately
deferred rather than shipping another unaudited preset.

\section{Matched Phase-A measurement}

Fixture: $n=16$, $|C|=|D|=256$, $s=64$, slice budget 320, source seed 1,
gadget seed 2, sandwich seed 1.  Every arm has the identical sandwich SHA-256
\code{17609cdc...c48e7a}.  Frozen stores are the identical Aug-10 regular and
curated m1--m11 stores.  Samplers use seed 7707 and 20,000 rounds.
All rows in this table use the default \emph{single-carrier} representation;
only the fold/fragment spelling changes.  These hit rates were not independently
remeasured for five-, six-, or seven-carrier artifacts.

``Reach'' is existential regular-store reachability of a gate through any
contiguous window of length 2--5 under control cap 2.  ``Dry MIX'' is raw
catalogue coverage and does not prove a usable committed splice. ``Dry COMP''

``Live'' is the new reliable counter: a nonidentity candidate was decoded,
placed, exhaustively verified, and actually spliced in a slot-2 round.
