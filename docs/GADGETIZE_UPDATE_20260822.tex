\noindent What changed in the gadgetization step (the
\texttt{gen\_sandwich\_gadget} single-carrier production path) across
this update cycle. The companion design note with the full rationale is
\texttt{docs/SWAP\_REFRESH\_REDESIGN.pdf}; the operational surface is
\texttt{docs/GSS\_MIX.md} (stage 1+2).

\section{What was replaced}
The aggregate-Gray fold era: time-invariant $[2,2,2,3]$ masks whose
cancellation in every fold's before/after XOR left the exact identity
$\mathrm{carrier}^{+} \oplus \mathrm{carrier}^{-} = \mathrm{src}^{+}
\oplus \mathrm{src}^{-}$ (100\% of linear source gates), the Gray
gather's linear operand-recovery witness, and a zero-slice guard at the
input port only.

\section{New mechanisms}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Symmetric junk-half port guards}: both ports carry
independent draws of one slice-guard generator (identity exactly on the
zero band slice, every nonzero slice perturbs the data, targets confined
to the low forward-junk half).
\item \textbf{Per-gate mask swap-with-refresh}: at every fold the target
and one random control each retire one base-degree monomial and gain a
fresh draw (verbatim moves and polarity re-rolls are provably unsound);
the target-side inject is strictly interior to the fragment stream.
\item \textbf{Gray declined; expanded fold + selective ladder (cap 3)}:
the gather materializes an operand's mask sum as an accumulator segment
pair, so swap mode takes the expanded fold; the ladder re-spells wide
fragments into g57/CNOT, borrowing scratch from live band variables.
\item \textbf{Target-stable commuting shuffle}: same-target XOR writes
keep emission order, making the interior interleave exact.
\item \textbf{Relocation-coupled refresh}: every relocation refreshes
one monomial of each moved value.
\item \textbf{Tail hygiene}: route-home before the strip (carrying the
band placement map); every registry discharged (the telescope lemma ---
an undischarged registry breaks reverse evaluation); discharge helpers
are live band variables via \texttt{loc}; fills source the low half.
\item \textbf{Split-channel epoch refills}: band-only linear pivot;
carrier sources only inside degree-2 product terms.
\item \textbf{Peephole cleanup}: commutable identical pairs cancelled to
a fixpoint ($-2$ to $-3.5\%$); the deliberate mask redundancy is
collision-guarded against any function-preserving local pass.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Contract changes}
The composite preserves the sandwich on the \emph{upper data half only}
(payload contract; the closing guard perturbs the junk half), and is
\emph{reverse-honest}: the reversed gadget on $(a, 0, \text{zero band})$
yields $D^{-1}(a)$ on the upper half --- one artifact evaluates $C$
forward and $D^{-1}$ backward. Both properties are verified at
generation time. \texttt{PROD\_SWAP=0 PROD\_CLOSE\_SLICE=0} reproduces
the prior Gray stream byte-for-byte.

\section{Measured properties}
flip\_match: zero body matches in both orientations (residual: 0--2
exact tail-seam windows per seed at $\ge 99.4\%$ depth, plus occasional
port-boundary fill coincidences). \texttt{segment\_deduce}: zero
interior-cut equations at degree 1 and windowed degree 2, both
directions. Inputs$\leftrightarrow$outputs affine rank full (257/257).
Affine-predictor heatmap flat at the 0.5 ceiling. Forward and reverse
verifies green at every size; gadget-family tests 140/140. Phase-A
digestibility (100k-move server probe): splice rate 56.5\% vs the 53.7\%
baseline, curated share 88.7\% vs 87.3\%, 6.28 bits/splice; the g57
share \emph{rises} 46.6\% $\to$ 58.5\% through phase A; the compression
channel succeeds on 2.1\% of attempts.

\section{Known residuals and cautions}
The per-seed tail-seam window flicker (accepted; phase A rewrites that
region first); \texttt{PROD\_POST\_FRAGMENT} re-opens broad linear
leakage under swap mode (off-limits until audited); multi-carrier
presets lack the output-port hygiene; mid-body refill balance is a
measured property, not a theorem.
