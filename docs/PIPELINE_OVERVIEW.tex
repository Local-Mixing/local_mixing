The pipeline's one free input is $n$, the wire count of the secret
computation $C$. Everything else is a library convention (a function of
$n$), a validated preset, or a measured calibration --- this document says
which, and why.

\section{Construction: sliced sandwich + gadgetization}

Sample a fresh random \g\ circuit $C$ on $n$ wires; build the sliced
sandwich $A$ on $2n$ wires (random $D$, keyed slice interleave, N-column
float) with $A(x,0)=(\mathrm{junk}, C(x))$ on the zero slice; apply the
selected zero-slice gadgetization and a final commuting shuffle.
\texttt{product-2223} is the production default and uses the diversified CG
menu, nonlinear RGs, and product-share masks. The experimental
\texttt{nonlinear193} and \texttt{nonlinear291} modes use two five-wire E
shares and canonical 193/291 r57 bodies, subject to the u16 capacity check in
\doc{GSS\_MIX.md} (\doc{SLICED\_SANDWICH.md},
\doc{NEW\_GADGETIZE.md}, \doc{NONLINEAR\_RG\_CG\_MENU.md}).

\section{Phase A: the DB re-encoding mix}

\texttt{fmix --gss --phase-a --profile}: a move walk whose DB moves
re-spell windows against the frozen store (MIX grows-if-needed, COMP
strictly shrinks) under a layer-2 size profile; \g-word twists supply
state-frame rotation. Phase A is deliberately \emph{\g-preserving}: its
job is re-encoding depth and spelling diversity, not structure breaking
(\doc{POSTMIX\_MANUAL.md} \S2, \doc{FMIX\_PHASE\_A.md},
\doc{FMIX\_LAYER1.md}, \doc{FMIX\_LAYER2.md}).

\begin{table}[H]\small

\toprule
knob & value & rationale \\
\midrule
size profile & $3,\,3{+}H,\,3{+}H{+}20,\,R,\,1{+}\frac{R-1}{2}$ (default $3,33,53,2,1.5$) & expansion fixed at 3 effs (transport happens during growth --- short, steep ramp); hold $H{=}30$ at $R{=}2$ is the re-encoding workhorse; compression fixed at 20 effs, ending halfway back ($1.5\times$) to keep spelling diversity a full return would spend (\doc{FMIX\_LAYER2.md}) \\

\section{Phase B: structure breaking}

Phase B's reframed job (2026-08-04) is \textbf{anti-inversion}: break the
\g\ structure with absolute spread --- not state mixing (fmix alone hides
nothing; hiding is sandwich-borne). No DB, no swap-family twists.

\textbf{Part 1 --- the split stage} (\texttt{--split}, shipped defaults).
One move to \g\ exhaustion: presplit a random \g\ into (CNOT/NCNOT,
2-control AND); with $p_{\mathrm{join}}=0.8$ wrap an \emph{absorbed}
pure-NOT twist on its target wire --- both brackets flip a control
polarity (zero synthetic gates), the segment's $w$-reading pins flip,
segment \g s force-split --- then one cross from the AND piece. Bracket
side drawn $\propto$ remaining circuit length (kills the short-span spike;
squared suppression); bracket = farthest of $k{=}2$ samples. Growth
$= 1+$ comp-fraction (measured $\times1.7$--$2.0$); output is dead-even
CNOT/NCNOT + $1{:}2{:}1$ ANDs, zero comp; coverage canary-verified
absolute. Spec \doc{FMIX\_SPLIT\_TWIST.md}; measurements and the
bracket-draw A/B in \doc{SPLIT\_TWIST\_REPORT.pdf} and
\doc{reports/split\_trials\_20260805/RESULTS.md}.

\textbf{Part 2 --- the crossing walk} (resumes the split state). Pure
crossings under the thermostat; the 2026-08-05 X-panel set every knob:

\begin{table}[H]\small
\begin{tabular}{L{3.0cm} L{3.4cm} L{8.2cm}}
\toprule
knob & value & rationale \\
\midrule
damping & $b=3$, $c=1$ & heavy damping EQUALIZES: median descendants up, tail down --- the only knob that lifts the median (judge spread by MEDIAN absolute descendants/span; the Yule tail makes means dishonest); $b>3$ probed: plateau by $b\approx3.5$ (single-seed deltas $\le0.006$); $b=3$ kept \\
temperature & target/25 & second-order; medium keeps arrival clean \\
target \texttt{--xr} & 2 (2.5 = max-spread) & frontier at the arrival peak: realized $1.63/1.84/2.01/2.25\times$ $\rightarrow$ frac($\ge$3 desc) $0.49/0.56/0.60/0.62$, median span $360/464/552/649$ --- decelerating; 2 clears median $\ge 3$ with margin \\
budget & $6\times$ target; \textbf{stop at arrival} & both median metrics PEAK at the damped equilibrium and the constant-size hold ERODES them (frac$\ge$3: $0.555\to0.498$ over $+20$M moves) --- the hold moves spread from the median to the tail; no moves-for-size trade exists past arrival \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The $2/3$ bar on frac($\ge$3) is out of expansion's reach; the next lever
is the min-dgen cross-shot bias (planned), not more growth.

\section{fcompress: the final pass and honesty check}

The attacker-computable greedy compressor, run whole-function
(\texttt{--live-wires all}), as both the final size pass and the honesty
metric: the residual is incompressibility \emph{earned} by mixing ---
healthy mixed material lands $\gtrsim 90\%$, vs $83\%$ for raw split
artifacts (\doc{POSTMIX\_MANUAL.md} \S3). Tool defaults; nothing tuned
per run.
