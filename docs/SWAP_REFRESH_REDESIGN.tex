\noindent
Redesign of the single-carrier production gadgetizer (the
\texttt{gen\_sandwich\_gadget} sandwich path). The aggregate-Gray fold is
replaced by a per-gate \emph{mask swap-with-refresh}; both ports carry an
independently drawn \emph{junk-half zero-slice guard}; the tail is
leak-hygienic; and the composite is \emph{reverse-honest}: the same
artifact evaluates $C(x)$ forward and $D^{-1}(a)$ backward, each on its
own zero slice --- the gadget-level mirror of the sandwich's symmetry.

\section{Goal}

No $\mathrm{GF}(2)$-linear equation should hold in which some variables
are wire segments of the source (sandwich) circuit and others are wire
segments of the gadgetized circuit --- except through the public port
boundary, which any correct implementation exhibits. The prior
construction failed this three measured ways: the endpoint cancellation
(time-invariant masks cancel in every fold's before/after XOR;
\texttt{flip\_match}: 100\% of linear source gates), the aggregate-Gray
operand witness ($B = c_b \XOR u^{-} \XOR u^{+} \XOR \kappa$), and the
one-sided zero-slice port. Adopted during the redesign: the construction
must be \emph{symmetric under reversal}, and the stream must remain
\emph{predominantly g57/CNOT} for phase-A digestion.

\section{The construction}

\noindent Layout under \texttt{production\_single()}
(\texttt{PROD\_SWAP=0 PROD\_CLOSE\_SLICE=0} restores the prior Gray
stream byte-for-byte):
\begin{center}\small
[$P$ junk-guard] [$W_0$ fill] [inject] [folds + swap/reloc churn]
[route home] [strip \textsc{all}] [$F'$ fill] [$Q$ junk-guard]
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Per-gate mask swap-with-refresh}: at every fold the target
and one random control each retire one base-degree monomial and gain a
fresh draw (a verbatim move leaks by $\mathrm{GF}(2)$ conservation, a
polarity re-roll by low-degree difference). The target-side inject is
strictly interior to the fold's fragment stream; a target-stable
commuting shuffle preserves per-wire write order; every relocation also
refreshes one monomial of each moved value. Gray modes are declined.
\item \textbf{Symmetric ports}: $P$ and $Q$ are independent draws of one
generator --- identity exactly on the zero band slice, every nonzero
slice perturbs the data, targets confined to the \textsc{low}
(forward-junk) data half. $Q$ fires forward into the junk half; $P^{-1}$
fires backward into the same half; neither touches the half where the
live payload of its direction emerges.
\item \textbf{Reverse honesty (the telescope lemma)}: a fold reading
value $v$ compensates $v$'s mask fragments against the carrier's
reverse-time content --- the XOR of the emissions \emph{after} the fold
--- which telescopes to $v$'s fold-time slots exactly when the final
registry is stripped. So \emph{every} value's registry is discharged at
the tail; keeping the junk half masked (an earlier revision) corrupted
$D^{-1}$. Verified bit-sliced at every size, forward and reverse, plus a
permanent reverse verify in \texttt{gen\_sandwich\_gadget}.
\item \textbf{Tail hygiene} (each item closed a measured
\texttt{flip\_match} class): route-home precedes the strip and carries
the band-placement map; the constant-discharge helpers are live band
\emph{variables} via \texttt{loc} (the raw band wire range holds
displaced carrier states); both band fills source from the low data
half; epoch refills are band-sourced only (the low half is the payload's
birthplace mid-circuit); the ladder borrows band variables, never live
carriers.
\end{itemize}

\section{Verification status ($n=128$, fresh random seeds)}

Function forward \emph{and} reverse: bit-sliced verifies pass; 318/318
tests. Inputs$\leftrightarrow$outputs: affine rank of $\{1,x,y\}$ full
($257/257$). \texttt{segment\_deduce} (degree 1 and restricted degree-2
windows, both directions, fresh-sample verified): zero interior-cut
equations. Affine-predictor heatmap vs $C$: flat at the $0.5$ ceiling,
port corners only, indistinguishable from the Gray control plate.
\texttt{flip\_match} (kwin 12/30/60, 8192 samples): body and nonlinear
classes at zero in both orientations. Residual: 0--2 exact \emph{seam
windows} per build (seed-dependent; 6/2/0/5 matched source gates over
four seeds, all $\ge 99.4\%$ depth) formed by mid-group cuts across the
route/strip seam, plus occasional port-boundary coincidences (early
$W_0$ / late $F'$ fill products over public port values equal to
early-C / late-D gate deltas). The seam is the region phase-A mixing
rewrites most heavily; driving the gadget-level flicker to zero at every
seed would need per-wire alternation of fresh material through the seam
--- an open refinement.
