\section{What was measured (the columns)}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Stage 6 v2 (C-aware)}: each round plans blocks that avoid cutting through exposed C values, resynthesizes every block from its ANF with two seeds and keeps a replacement only if it is smaller and not leakier; rounds repeat until two consecutive rounds compress jointly by less than 2\%. The smallest round output is F, the delivered circuit. Its *census* (validated: every witness re-checked on 8,192 fresh plus structured inputs) counts the deeper internal C births (886 of 936 for the random C, 884 for the balanced C; the 50-52 excluded are degree-2 functions of public inputs or outputs) present directly (verbatim up to NOT at some cut) /\allowbreak{} as an affine combination of simultaneously live wires at some cut, written d/\allowbreak{}a.
\item \textbf{Raw ledger on F}: C-blind observers on F itself (direct ledger plus affine-window observers at ranks 10 and 12), labeled against C afterwards and audited; R/\allowbreak{}O = births found with \textgreater{}= 2 distinct representations (repeated) /\allowbreak{} exactly one (once).
\item \textbf{Narrow portfolio on F}: ten C-blind window rewrites of F followed by the same observers: factor rewrites with 125-, 250-, 500- and 1,000-gate windows, factor 125 shifted by 63, factor 125 on a wire-permuted circuit, pairs 125, and three payload-view cases (factor 125, factor 250, delta 250); the 500/\allowbreak{}1,000-gate windows were added in this campaign because a stage-5 fire spans \textasciitilde{}1,200 gates, and 2,000/\allowbreak{}4,000-gate windows were added later ("+2" in the count). The same portfolio was run on the shortened circuits, and on the 5.2-eff remixed circuit before and after its resynthesis for some arms.
\item \textbf{Blind direct}: C-unaware adaptive resynthesis of F (same machinery as stage 6 with an empty census, one seed, keep-smaller, iterated to a 1\% two-round rule, the previous campaign's attacker rule), with the validated census of its plateau P.
\item \textbf{Remix 5.2 /\allowbreak{} remix 2}: stage-3 remixing of F with the frozen store for 5.2 or 2 effective units of work (the circuit grows about 2.1x /\allowbreak{} 1.7x), then the blind resynthesis of the remix with its census.
\item \textbf{Shorten}: extra C-blind compression of P (fcompress transport compression over function-preserving reorderings, a 128-gate-cap blind pass, fcompress, a 48-gate-cap pass, fcompress; verified against P and the payload contract) with the validated census of the result; motivated by the finding that blind plateaus are not size floors.
\item \textbf{Candidates C/\allowbreak{}all}: a C-blind scan on 8,192 honest samples counts the internal functions born at least twice (up to NOT) that are not affine in the inputs, then labels them against C: "deeper C births among them /\allowbreak{} all such candidates".
\item \textbf{Degree-2 replicas mid-circuit}: at interior cuts (depths 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60), how many of the 64 payload outputs are degree \textless{}= 2 polynomials of the wires live at the cut (C-blind; the degree-2 span is kept below the 36,864 samples so the test is non-vacuous). The direct payload replicas near the output (54-56 of 64 in F for most arms) are the sandwich's own copies and are not reported.
\item \textbf{Sandwich classes} (rust family): the census run against the whole 128-wire sandwich as source (every gate with target \textless{} 64), classifying each sandwich gate as C g57 (936 internal, C's outputs excluded), D g57 (951), C/\allowbreak{}D transition CNOT (the 64 copy CNOTs carry C's outputs; counted only when a replica interval starts before 90\% of the circuit), slicing CNOT (262), slicing Toffoli (491).
\item \textbf{Tally}: deeper C births exposed in any circuit produced or measured since the campaign started on F (raw and narrow ledgers of F and of its derived circuits, every blind round output, both remixes and their rounds); strong = \textgreater{}= 2 supports in at least one circuit. No tallied birth is exposed only in the last 10\% of a circuit (checked).
\item \textbf{Inversion}: C-blind attempts to compute a preimage of the all-zero payload: a greedy structural walk (backward from the payload outputs, forward from the inputs; a meet would reconstruct C) and a CryptoMiniSat preimage search with a 24 h wall cap (8 threads), each on F and on P.
\end{itemize}

\section{Findings}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The 1,000-gate window is the strongest test everywhere, and larger windows add nothing.} On the stage-6 outputs the 1,000-gate factor rewrite finds 224-345 deeper births for the Codex-generator arms where the 125-gate cases find 40-130; 2,000 and 4,000 gates give the same counts (base 232/\allowbreak{}232, skip5 350/\allowbreak{}348, bal 283/\allowbreak{}279, rust 284/\allowbreak{}284, rust2 85/\allowbreak{}85). The earlier campaign's portfolio, which stopped at 250 gates, under-reported exposure two to three times; with the long windows, base, skip3, skip5, bal, k24 and rust all end between 329 and 435 deeper births exposed, i.e. a third to a half of C.
\item \textbf{rust2 is the only design that resists the window rewrites}: 128 narrow /\allowbreak{} 159 total on F (85 in the long windows), three times fewer than every other arm, and its blind lines expose 8-29 instead of 17-61. rust (turnover alone, weak interleave) is as exposed as base (387 narrow, 407 total), so the effective fire interleaving is the element that works, not the turnover.
\item \textbf{Skipping stage 3 does not reduce exposure and removes stage 6's leverage}: without stage 3's identities the stage-6 synthesizer cannot compress (skip3 and rust2\_skip3 plateau at their stage-5 size with 0/\allowbreak{}0 census), the narrow portfolio exposes 390 (skip3) and 51 on F (rust2\_skip3), and the shortened circuits are the most exposed of all (515 and 323). Skipping stage 5 (skip5) halves the circuit but leaks like base (412 narrow, 419 total).
\item \textbf{The balanced C does not help against the C-blind observers} (bal: 404 narrow, 435 total, the highest of the Codex-generator arms), consistent with the per-read exposure model of the previous campaign.
\item \textbf{k-max 24 changes nothing}: stage 5 produced 19 gates above 12 controls and k24 tracks base within 1\% at every stage (F 2,735,148 vs 2,720,511; narrow 308 vs 315).
\item \textbf{Compression attacks expose little by themselves but shortening and remixing move the picture}: the blind direct plateaus expose 0-22 births (census) and are not size floors; fcompress-plus-small-window shortening compresses P by 25-47\% and the shortened circuits are more exposed to the window rewrites than F for every arm; remixing then recompressing exposes 23-61 (census) and, for base, 375 births through the narrow portfolio of the remix final versus 158 on the remix itself.
\item \textbf{No preimage from any inversion attempt}; the C-blind candidate haystack is 9k-209k repeated non-affine functions per circuit with at most 8 deeper C births among them.
\item \textbf{The payload is not replicated at degree 2 anywhere inside any circuit} (0 of 64 at all tested depths for all arms), unlike the old baseline's 36-50, which closes the midpoint-replication avenue of the preimage campaign for this stage-2 family.
\item \textbf{The sandwich leaks through its slicing gates, not through C}: on rust2's F, 0 of 936 C births but 9 of 951 D births, 22 of 262 slicing CNOT outputs and 38 of 491 slicing Toffoli outputs are directly present; the same pattern holds for rust and rust2\_skip3.
\end{itemize}

\section{Status and caveats}

Still running at the time of writing: the blind remix lines of rust and rust2, rust2\_skip3's resilience (its remixes were produced on the Mac Pro against the local store after the server's store was deleted to free 299 GB; the store is re-fetched automatically when the campaign ends), the remix gauntlets (rust2 and base first), the 2,000/\allowbreak{}4,000-gate windows of the arms not yet covered, the shortened-circuit portfolios of rust and rust2\_skip3, and the remaining SAT inversion runs. Caveats: one seed and one C per arm (the Rust arms share base's); the attacker rules (1\% stop) and the ten standard cases are the previous campaign's for comparability; the stage-6 drivers of base, skip3, skip5, bal, k24, rust and the first rounds of rust2 ran the original driver, later rounds of rust2 and all of rust2\_skip3 the revised one (identical results for bit-identical inputs except for the dense-window budget and the late single seed); the degree-2 scan tests only depths 0.40-0.60.
